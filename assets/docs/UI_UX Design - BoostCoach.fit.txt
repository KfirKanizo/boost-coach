UI/UX Design Specification: BoostCoach.fit
Lead Architect: Kfir Kanizo
Date: August 2026


1. Design System & Brand Identity
The visual identity is crafted to reduce cognitive load and convey a modern, frictionless, and energetic experience. The design relies heavily on a dark theme to prevent glare during workouts and to make color-coded feedback instantly recognizable.


Color Palette


* Background (Primary): Deep Charcoal (#121212) - Used for the main app background to save battery and reduce eye strain.
* Surface (Secondary): Dark Grey (#1E1E1E) - Used for cards, bottom sheets, and the navigation bar.
* Action/Success (Accent): Neon Green (#00E676) - Used for the main "EXECUTE" button, completed Boost checkmarks, correct posture tracking lines, and success icons.
* Energy Map (Streak): Warm Orange/Amber (#FFB300 to #FF6D00) - Used exclusively for the daily streak bubbles. The color becomes more vibrant as the user completes more Boosts.
* Warning/Error: Crimson Red (#FF1744) - Used for incorrect posture feedback in the camera view and destructive actions.
* Text (Primary): Pure White (#FFFFFF) - Used for high-contrast readability on dark backgrounds.
* Text (Secondary): Light Grey (#B0B0B0) - Used for subtitles, timestamps, and placeholder text.


Typography


* English Font: Inter or Roboto - Selected for its clean, geometric, and highly legible structure on mobile screens.
* Hebrew Font: Rubik - Matches the modern, rounded aesthetic of the English font.
* Usage Rules: Heavy weights (Bold/Black) are reserved for the countdown timer and main headers. Regular weights are used for Coach chat bubbles and descriptions. The timer in the camera view uses a monospaced variant to prevent text jitter as numbers count down.

2. Global Layout & Navigation Architecture
The application employs a flat, hierarchy-free navigation model to ensure zero-friction usability.


* Bottom Navigation Bar: A fixed, semi-transparent bar containing exactly three icons: The Flow (Home), The Coach (Chat), and Profile (Settings).
* No Hamburger Menus: All primary actions are accessible within a single tap.
* Environment Toggle: A pill-shaped toggle button pinned to the top of the Home screen, allowing seamless switching between environments (e.g., "Home" vs. "Gym").


3. Core Screens & Interactive Controls
3.1 The Flow (Home Screen)
* The Energy Map (Header): A horizontal, horizontally scrollable mosaic of circular bubbles representing the last 14 days.
* Energy Map States: Missed days are outlined in dim grey. Completed days glow with a solid Warm Orange fill. Today's bubble pulses gently until the first Boost is completed.
* Boost Cards (Body): A vertical list of large, easily tappable cards representing the day's exercises. Each card displays the exercise name, target reps/time, and a "Swap" icon (🔄) on the right side.
* Main Action Button: A massive, Floating Action Button (FAB) anchored above the navigation bar, filled with Neon Green, labeled "EXECUTE". Tapping this immediately opens the camera for the first pending Boost.


3.2 Smart Swap (Bottom Sheet)
* Trigger: The user taps the Swap icon on a Boost card.
* Display: A smooth Bottom Sheet slides up over The Flow, dimming the background.
* Content: A simple header ("Why swap?") and three large, pill-shaped buttons: "No Equipment", "Muscle Sore", and "Need Variety".
* Interaction: Tapping an option triggers a quick haptic bump, immediately closes the sheet, and the original Boost card performs a 3D "flip" animation to reveal the newly assigned exercise.


________________
3.3 The Studio (Camera Execution HUD)
This is a full-screen, immersive augmented reality view. The bottom navigation bar is hidden.


* Skeleton Overlay: Thin, semi-transparent white lines connect the user's joints (landmarks).
* Tracking States: When the user reaches the correct depth (e.g., bottom of a squat), the specific joint lines snap to Neon Green. If the back rounds dangerously, the spine line snaps to Crimson Red and flashes.
* The HUD (Heads-Up Display): A massive, 120pt semi-transparent number sits centered at the top of the screen, counting down (e.g., "15", "14").
* Progress Bar: A thin Neon Green line at the very bottom of the screen fills up as the set progresses from 0% to 100%.


3.4 The Coach (AI Feedback Interface)
* Layout: Resembles a modern messaging app like WhatsApp.
* Summary Card: Pinned at the top of the chat, displaying today's total time and updated streak count.
* Chat Bubbles: The AI's messages are aligned left (Dark Grey bubbles). The user's replies are aligned right (Neon Green bubbles).
* Typing Indicator: Displays three animated dots when waiting for the LLM API response, reassuring the user that the system is processing.
3.5 Overlays & Dynamic States (Popups & Warnings)
* The Victory State (Success Overlay): Triggered immediately when the countdown timer hits 0. The background camera feed receives a heavy Gaussian blur. A massive, glowing Neon Green Checkmark (✓) springs into the center of the screen, accompanied by dynamic text (e.g., "Boost Completed! +1 Energy"). The overlay remains for 1.5 seconds before automatically routing the user back to The Flow.
* Environmental Warning Toast: A semi-transparent, Crimson Red floating pill at the top of The Studio screen. Displays critical Edge AI errors without stopping the camera feed entirely (e.g., "⚠️ Low Light Detected" or "↔️ Step Back for Full Body Tracking").
* Progressive Profiling Prompt: A subtle, slide-in card (Bottom-Up or Toast notification) appearing in The Flow or The Coach. It features a conversational question ("What's your current weight?") with a single numerical input field and a "Save" button, maintaining the Dark Grey (#1E1E1E) surface color.


________________
4. Gamification, Micro-interactions & Feedback Loops
Feedback mechanisms are heavily utilized to build dopamine-driven habits without manual intervention.


* Mid-Exercise Success: Upon completing a single valid repetition, the system plays a subtle, crisp "Pop" sound and triggers a light haptic vibration. The on-screen counter ticks down immediately.
* Error State Notification: If form is broken, the counter pauses, a low-pitch "Buzzer" sound plays, the screen edges glow Crimson Red, and a text prompt appears briefly (e.g., "Keep your back straight!").
* Set Completion (The Victory State):

   * Trigger: The countdown reaches 0.
   * Visuals: The camera feed blurs instantly. A massive, glowing Neon Green Checkmark (✓) scales up from the center of the screen with a slight bounce effect (spring animation).
   * Audio/Haptics: A triumphant "Level Up" chime plays, accompanied by a heavy, double-pulse haptic vibration.
   * Transition: After 1.5 seconds, the screen automatically slides down, returning the user to The Flow. The completed Boost card is now grayed out with a green checkmark, and the next Boost is cued up.
